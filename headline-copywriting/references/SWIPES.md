# SWIPES: proven headlines, verbatim

Compiled 2026-10-06. Part A is hero text fetched live from homepages today (curl/WebFetch). Part B is the Victor Schwab '100 Great Headlines' list (copied from zettasphere.com/100-great-headlines, which reproduces it). Part C is get-clients offers. Word counts are by whitespace split. Where a site blocked fetching (Upwork, Fiverr, Canva, Lovable, Midjourney, Gumroad-adjacent Cloudflare pages) it is omitted rather than guessed. Stripe's page served in Dutch, so it is omitted. Instantly and Flair show text exactly as served (Flair's 'productphoots' looks like their typo). Headlines are quoted exactly even where the original used an em dash.
